## Inspiration

This mod was born as a private showcase — a localhost-only gateway that lets a trusted friend
send small payloads into your own Minecraft session. It borrows ideas (and a warning banner)
from the classic remote-control / RCE mods and experiments that circulated in the Minecraft
community, reimplemented from scratch for safety-first, loopback-only, per-IP trust.
